# Import

**Import** brings a book original into your **Vault** so that Lekto can render it.

## Supported formats

| Format | What to expect |
| ------ | -------------- |
| EPUB   | First-class support. Headings, chapters and reading order are preserved. |
| TXT    | First-class support. Plain text is read as one continuous book. |
| PDF    | Best-effort text extraction. A scanned or heavily styled PDF may extract poorly or not at all. |

If a PDF does not extract usefully, try an EPUB or a TXT of the same text.

## Importing a book

Choose Import, pick a file, and Lekto reads it, parses it to text, stores both the
parsed text and the **Book original**, and adds the book to your [Library](/library).

Importing is an occasional action: it reports its progress and surfaces a failure
instead of blocking the app. A book you have already imported stays in your Vault;
the **Book original** travels with an export so the book can be restored on
another device.

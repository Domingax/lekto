# Vocabulary

A **Vocabulary entry** is a word or phrase you have saved, with its translation, the
**Context sentence** you met it in, and a **Mastery level**.

## Saving a word

Open the lookup panel from a word token and tap a **Mastery level** chip: the
level is the save. Lekto keeps the sentence the word was cut from, so you can
review it in context rather than in isolation.

Entries are keyed by the word's **Word key**: two spellings that share a **Lemma**
share one key, so saving *mangeais* updates the entry for *manger* rather than
creating a second one.

## Managing your vocabulary

The Vocabulary list — the second section — shows every saved word with its level,
translation and context sentence, and the count beside the title says how many
words you have saved. The translation is read from the **Dictionary pack** installed for the
word's language and your **Translation language**, so installing or updating a pack
refreshes what you see. When no pack answers — none is installed, or the pack does
not know the word — the list falls back to the translation saved with the word.

You can search the list by spelling, lemma, translation or context sentence, and
filter it by **Mastery level**. A search that finds nothing says which words it
looked at and offers to clear itself.

## Changing a word's level

Tap a word in the list — anywhere on the row — and a sheet opens with the word,
its translation, the sentence you met it in, and the five **Mastery level**s.
Tap the level the word has reached: the sheet stays open on the new level and the
row behind it changes. If a level filter is on and the word no longer matches it,
the row leaves the list, which tells you the change landed.

This is the same level the reader writes, so the word's colour in the book changes
too, and the change syncs like any other vocabulary edit.

On a wide desktop window the word opens in a column beside the list, the same shape
the reader uses for its lookup, so the list never narrows under your eyes.

Deleting an entry returns the word to unsaved in the reader. There is no undo, so
the delete asks first: swipe the row to the left on Android, or move the pointer
over it on desktop and use the bin that appears, then confirm.

## Mastery levels

Each entry carries a level from 0 (unknown) to 4 (known); levels 0–3 are
highlighted in the reader and level 4 is shown as normal text. Setting a level
recolours the word everywhere it appears. See [Reading](/reading) for the scale.

Hear a saved word aloud with [Pronunciation](/pronunciation).

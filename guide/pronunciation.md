# Pronunciation

**Pronunciation** plays a selected word or phrase aloud through your device's own
speech engine. Nothing is sent to a network service, and nothing is stored.

## How it works

Open the lookup panel from a word token and choose Listen. Lekto asks the platform
for a voice in the book's language and speaks the selection.

## When there is no voice

Lekto uses the voices your device already has installed. If a language has no
installed voice, the panel says so honestly rather than failing silently or leaving
you waiting. On some platforms you can install an extra voice in the system
settings, then try again.

Pronunciation is one of several things the lookup panel offers; see
[Word lookup](/word-lookup) for the rest.

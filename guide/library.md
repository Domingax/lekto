# Library

The **Library** is the list of **Books** in your **Vault**. It is the app's
starting screen and the way back from a reading session, on Android and on
desktop alike.

## What you see

The three books you read most recently lead the list, under **Continue
reading**, the most recent first. A book joins that section as soon as you turn
a page in it, and turning back to its first page does not remove it. Below them,
every other book — including one you have not opened yet — is listed by title.
When you have not read anything yet, there is no section: the list is just your
books, by title.

Each row is one book, showing its title, its format and its file name. A Library
with no books yet says so and points you at **Import**, the button at the bottom
of the screen.

The Library is a view of the Vault, not the storage itself: the books are one
part of the Vault, and your vocabulary and reading progress live in the same
Vault beside them. For where that data lives and how to move it, see
[Vault and sync](/vault-and-sync).

## Finding a book

When the Library holds at least one book, a **Search** field sits under the
title. Type part of a title and the list narrows to the books that contain it:
matching ignores capitals and accents, so *emile* finds *Émile*. Only titles are
searched, not file names.

While you are searching, the **Continue reading** section disappears and the
matches are listed by title. An empty field shows the Library as usual. If
nothing matches, Lekto names what you typed and offers **Clear search**; the
cross beside the field clears it too.

The search is a view of your Library, not a setting: leaving the Library — to
read a book, or to open Vocabulary, Languages or Settings — clears it, and it is
never saved to your **Vault**.

## Opening a book

Tap a book to open a [reading session](/reading). Lekto restores your **Reading
position** automatically, so you continue where you left off; see
[Reading](/reading) for how that position survives a change of screen or font.

## Deleting a book

Each row has a **Delete** action. Lekto asks you to confirm first — *Delete
« title »? This cannot be undone.* — and only then removes the **Book**: its
original file, the text derived from it and your **Reading position** for it. If
sync is on, the deletion travels to your other devices instead of the book coming
back; see [Vault and sync](/vault-and-sync) for how a deletion is remembered.

Deleting a book is permanent, so Lekto offers it only from the Library, never
from inside a [reading session](/reading).

## Bringing books in

**Import** brings a book into the Library. See [Import](/import) for the formats
and what each supports.

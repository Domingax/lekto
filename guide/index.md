## Download

Lekto runs on Android and on the desktop (Linux, macOS and Windows).

- **Android** — [download the latest APK](https://github.com/Domingax/lekto/releases/latest).
- **Desktop** — [download the latest release](https://github.com/Domingax/lekto/releases/latest).

The download links point at the repository's latest release; they stay dormant
until the first release ships. New to Lekto? Start with [Getting started](/getting-started).

## What is Lekto?

Lekto is an immersive reader for language learners. You import a book you want to
read, and Lekto colours its words by how well you already know them. Tap a word to
look it up, save it, and set how well you know it. Over time the page turns from a
wall of unfamiliar words into a text you can read.

Everything is local-first: there is no account and no backend. All the data you
author or import lives in a **Vault** on your own device, and there is no Lekto
server anywhere between you and your books.

Lekto is free and open source, released under the AGPL-3.0 licence. The source
lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/Domingax/lekto).
